# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Easy Cartoonify project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Positive behaviors include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, and online community channels.
